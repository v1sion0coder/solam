Solam BETA

Your music, behind liquid glass.

A free music app with no ads, animated themes, synced lyrics and artists you can follow. Upload your own songs, share playlists with friends, and get shown on the Home page.

This is a beta version, so some things may not work yet.

Windows 10 and 11

Download for Windows

iPhone

Open Solam for iPhone
  1. Open that link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Solam from your Home Screen, like any other app.

It isn't on the App Store yet. On iPhone, volume is set with your phone's buttons.

Android

Open Solam for Android
  1. Open that link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open Solam from your Home screen, like any other app.

What's inside

  • Free songs from independent artists and legal free albums
  • 30-second previews of famous artists
  • Bring your playlists and favourite artists from other music services
  • Comments, shared playlists and song links with QR codes
  • Animated themes in English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese and German

Why Windows shows a warning

Windows warns about new apps that don't yet have a paid digital signature and a download history, which is the case for Solam. If you trust this site, click More info, then Run anyway.

You can check that your download is the original. In PowerShell, run Get-FileHash Solam-Setup.exe and compare it with:
