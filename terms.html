Beta version

Solam is a beta. Features may change or stop working, and data could be lost. Solam is provided as it is, with no guarantees. Keep your own copies of your music.

Age

You must be 13 or older to upload music. If the law where you live sets a higher age for using online services on your own, you need your parent's or guardian's permission.

Your uploads

  • Only upload music you made or have the right to share. No stolen music.
  • No illegal, hateful or sexually explicit content involving minors, and nothing that harms other people.
  • You keep the rights to your music. You let Solam store it and play it to other people in the app until you delete it.
  • Every song is reviewed before it goes public. Songs can be refused or removed at any time.

Music from other sources

Albums under Free to share come from netlabels on the Internet Archive under Creative Commons or public domain terms, and each one shows the license its uploader gave it. Solam filters these but can't guarantee every label is correct, so please report any mistake. Solam also shows music from Audius and the Internet Archive, 30-second previews and artist information from Deezer, and lyrics from LRCLIB. They belong to their owners, and Solam doesn't control them.

Connectors

Connectors imports files you download yourself from other music services. Solam isn't connected to or endorsed by Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or SoundCloud, and it can't play your full music from them.

Reporting a song

If a song is stolen or harmful, use the Report option in the app or write to salam0support@gmail.com with the song name and why it's a problem.

Copyright

If a song uses your work without permission, open the … menu on the song and choose Report this song, then Report for copyright. Give your name, your email and what your work is. Reports are checked, and songs can be removed. Only send a report if it is true. Artists can delete their own songs at any time.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account. Accounts that break these rules can be removed.

Premium

Until Premium goes on sale, its features are free for everyone. Once it is on sale, those features need Premium. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying.

Premium is paid by bank transfer, one month at a time, and never renews by itself. It is switched on by hand after the payment is checked, usually within a day. Always include your payment reference. If your payment doesn't arrive or Premium doesn't switch on, or you want a refund, use the support form or write to salam0support@gmail.com. Your legal rights to cancel and get a refund still apply.

Solam AI

Solam AI can be wrong, so check anything important. It has a daily message limit. Don't use it for anything illegal or harmful.

Comments and shared playlists

Be kind. No hate, harassment, threats, spam or sexual content. Don't post other people's private information. Comments can be reported and removed, and accounts that break these rules can be removed too. Anyone with the link to a shared playlist can see it and add songs.

Changes

These terms may change as Solam grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
