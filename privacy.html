Privacy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. You can use Solam to listen without an account.

What Solam stores online, only if you create an account

  • Email and password: to sign you in. Your password is stored in scrambled form, and nobody can read it.
  • Favourites, playlists and followed artists: so they follow you to other PCs.
  • If you upload music: your real name (kept private), your public app name, your genre, your photos and your songs.
  • Reports: what you write when you report a song or comment.
  • Comments: what you write and the name you choose. Everyone can read them.
  • Shared playlists: their songs, and which accounts have joined them.
  • Premium: whether you have it and until when, plus your payment code. The Solam owner sees your email with that code, and their bank shows your name and account with your transfer.

What Solam stores without an account

  • Listener counts: when a song by a Solam artist plays, Solam saves a random number made by the app, the song and the date, so artists can see monthly listeners. It isn't linked to your account.
  • Support messages: what you write in the support form, and your email if you add it.

What Solam doesn't keep

  • Your date of birth is only used to check that you are 13 or older, on your own PC. It is never saved or sent.
  • Your Windows PC's sound: the moving visuals listen to it on your PC only. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • No ads, and your data isn't sold.

What stays on your device

Your settings, music folders, downloads and recently played songs.

Connectors

If you import a file from another music service, Solam reads it on your device and never uploads the file. It keeps only the song names, playlists and artists. Those are saved online only if you have an account, so they can follow you to other devices. To find songs, Solam sends their names to Deezer. You can remove an import at any time in Connectors.

Other services Solam contacts

To find and play music, Solam sends your searches and requests to Audius, the Internet Archive, Deezer and LRCLIB, which also see your internet address. Accounts and uploads are stored with Supabase on servers in the EU. The website is hosted by GitHub, and sign-up emails are sent by Supabase or Google.

Your choices

Delete your account in the app under Account, which removes your songs, photos, name and saved music from the server. For any other request, write to salam0support@gmail.com.

Кратко по-русски

Слушать можно без аккаунта. С аккаунтом хранятся почта, избранное и плейлисты, а при загрузке музыки — настоящее имя (скрыто), имя в приложении, фото и песни. Дата рождения не сохраняется. Рекламы нет, данные не продаются. Аккаунт можно удалить в приложении. Вопросы: salam0support@gmail.com.
